MediaInfo.Wrapper 26.10.0

MediaInfoLib 26.10, through MediaInfo.Native 26.10.0. Targets net4.0, net4.5, net481 (new). The asynchronous analyzer is in MediaInfo.Wrapper.Core only. Previous release: 26.1.0.

ADDED

- AudioStream.DynamicObjects, SubtitleStream.MaxCharactersPerLine.
- VideoStream.Rotation, ScanType, DisplayAspectRatio.
- More audio, subtitle, video and HDR format identifiers.
- P/Invoke declarations for the remaining MediaInfoLib exports.

CHANGED

- DTS:X reports 12 channels (7.1.4) instead of 8; its objects are in DynamicObjects.
- Five-channel Opus is 5.0, not 4.1.
- MP3 duration and bit rate are more accurate with encoder delay or a Xing header.
- MediaInfoList.Open honours InfoFileOptions.

FIXED

- Heap corruption (process exit 0xc0000374): strings owned by the native library were freed by the runtime.
- MediaInfoWrapper no longer passes a missing file to the native library.

Release notes: https://github.com/yartat/MP-MediaInfo/blob/master/docs/release-notes/26.10.0.md
